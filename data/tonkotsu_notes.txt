Tonkotsu ramen — Bachan's base recipe + my adjustments
=====================================================

Hana requested this for Oct 23 homecoming dinner

Bones: 3 lbs pork neck + 2 lbs femur (ask Fred Meyer butcher)
Boil time: 12 hours minimum. Bachan says 14. Last time I did 11
and it wasn't creamy enough.

Tare: soy + sake + mirin + kombu. My ratio is 3:1:1 + strip.
Bachan's is different but she won't tell me exactly.

Chashu: pork belly, roll and tie, sear all sides,
braise 2hrs in soy/mirin/sake/sugar. Slice thin when cold.

Eggs: 6.5 min boil, ice bath, marinate overnight in tare.
Brendan likes his 7 min. Compromise: make both.

Toppings: green onion, nori, sesame, bean sprouts,
menma (buy jarred, homemade is not worth it on a weeknight).

Start broth night before. Everything else day-of.
